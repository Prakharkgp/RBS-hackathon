

Following our discussion yesterday, here are my initial thoughts.

1. Priority criteria/events
Commercial Opportunities / Private Banking

Business or shareholding sale

Potential cash event, reinvestment opportunities, and wealth planning.

M&A / acquisition

Financing needs, liquidity management, and changes in net worth.

Fundraising / capital increase

Potential increase in the client's shareholding value and future liquidity event.

IPO / listing / delisting

Major liquidity event.

Special dividend or significant distribution

Potentially investable liquidity.

Significant real estate acquisition or disposal

Mortgage, financing, or reinvestment opportunities.

Creation of a new company, holding company, or subsidiary

Structuring, financing, and cash management opportunities.

Expansion into a new country

International banking, FX, and financing needs.

Appointment to a key position (CEO, CFO, Board member, etc.)

Opportunity for engagement and potential wealth evolution.

Significant acquisition or disposal of shares / threshold crossing

Change in wealth profile and potential cash event.
Corporate / Credit
New debt or financing.
Refinancing.
Significant change in revenue, earnings, or leverage.
Major acquisition.
Restructuring.
Financial deterioration or insolvency proceedings.
KYC / Client Knowledge
Change of shareholder or UBO.
New executive appointment.
Creation or dissolution of related entities.
Change of jurisdiction, headquarters, or corporate structure.
Significant modification of the ownership structure.
Risk / Compliance
Sanctions.
PEP status or appointment to a relevant public office.
Regulatory enforcement actions.
Significant litigation or legal proceedings.
Insolvency/liquidation.
Credible adverse media (fraud, corruption, financial crime, etc.).
2. Public sources to prioritize

For an initial prototype, I would suggest integrating the following sources first:

INPI / French National Business Register

French companies, directors, filings, accounts, and corporate changes.

BODACC

Incorporations, business sales/transfers, modifications, and insolvency proceedings.

Companies House

UK companies: directors, Persons with Significant Control (PSC), filing history, accounts, charges, and insolvencies.

AMF

Listed companies, major shareholding disclosures, insider transactions, sanctions, and regulatory disclosures.

SEC EDGAR

Extremely rich equivalent for US-listed companies.

EU Sanctions Map and official national/international sanctions lists

Compliance-related monitoring.

Corporate websites

Investor relations announcements, M&A activity, results, management changes, dividends, etc.

Reliable financial media

Useful for identifying events before official registries are updated.
3. Existing private sources or sources to consider

To benchmark against what the bank already has in place:

Moody's Orbis
Answers the question: "Who owns what?"
Particularly useful for linking an event detected on an indirectly owned company back to the correct client.
Mergermarket
Answers the question: "Who is buying/selling what?"
Highly valuable for M&A and liquidity event use cases.
Early intelligence on a business sale can generate substantial commercial value.
PitchBook
Answers the question: "Who is raising/investing?"
Complements Mergermarket for private companies, entrepreneurs, PE/VC firms, funding rounds, and investors.
Altrata / Wealth-X
More person-centric than company-centric.
Very relevant for UHNW prospecting and Private Banking client acquisition.
Additional regulatory and compliance-focused sources

LSEG World-Check (possibly already available via Jordan?)

Sanctions, PEPs, and risk intelligence.

LexisNexis (possibly already available on the Compliance side?)

Adverse media, due diligence, and international coverage.

SPARK/Interfax

Already used for certain Russian individuals and entities.
Interesting example of a geography-specific intelligence source.

Salamanca

Occasionally used when there is a strong commercial opportunity involving prospects or existing clients.
More suited for investigations and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) when a detected signal requires deeper human analysis.

Overall, I believe the most value will come from combining:

Official registries (INPI, BODACC, Companies House, AMF, SEC),
Commercial intelligence sources (Mergermarket, PitchBook),
Ownership mapping databases (Orbis),
Risk and compliance solutions (World-Check, LexisNexis),

to create a comprehensive event-monitoring capability covering commercial opportunities, KYC updates, and risk/compliance requirements.
